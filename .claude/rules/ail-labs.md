# AIL LABS Project Rules

- Treat AIL LABS as a B2B medical/aesthetic distribution and private-label operation.
- Audience: clinics, doctors, dermatologists, hospitals, PBF, pharmacy chains; do not frame as a consumer e-commerce store unless explicitly requested.
- Preserve business, legal, regulatory, and procurement constraints from current approved source documents.
- Supplier communication must not make purchase/payment commitments without explicit approval.
- Follow the user's procurement SOP and current approval rules; do not infer supplier acceptance.
- Separate verified regulatory facts from pending items. For BPOM, import, HS code, licensing, halal, and product claims, verify current authoritative sources before external communication.
- Never expose private supplier/customer data, account details, credentials, or internal strategy in public artifacts.
- Before production website/database/auth changes: inspect current state, propose minimal additive change, obtain approval, back up, validate, and report.
- Preserve the production catalog and database; do not perform destructive migrations or broad rewrites without approval.
