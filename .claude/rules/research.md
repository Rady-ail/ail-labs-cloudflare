# Research Rules

- Prefer official documentation, primary repositories, standards, and original papers.
- Check publication/update date for fast-changing claims.
- Verify license from the exact upstream `LICENSE` file, not from a secondary article.
- Label evidence: verified / likely / unverified.
- Cite claims near the relevant sentence.
- Compare competing approaches using criteria: reliability, security, maintenance, portability, cost, and user fit.
- Do not claim “best”, “expert-approved”, “MIT”, “secure”, or “production-ready” without adequate evidence.
